### Step 1 — Understand the numbers

In C major:

| Degree | Note | Chord   |
| ------ | ---- | ------- |
| I      | C    | C major |
| IV     | F    | F major |
| V      | G    | G major |

So:

**I = C**
**IV = F**
**V = G**

### Step 2 — Play each chord separately

Right hand:

* C = `C-E-G`
* F = `F-A-C`
* G = `G-B-D`

Don't worry about speed yet.

Play:

`C → F → G → C`

### Step 3 — Hold each chord

Play:

**C** → hold 4 counts
**F** → hold 4 counts
**G** → hold 4 counts
**C** → hold 4 counts

Do this 5 times.

### Step 4 — Reduce the gap

Now:

`C → F → G → C`

Try to change chords without completely stopping.

### Step 5 — Add rhythm

Play:

`C | F | G | C`

Each chord = 4 beats.

Then try:

`C | F | G | G`

Then resolve to C.

### 🎯 Goal

You should eventually be able to play:

**C → F → G → C**

smoothly without thinking about individual notes.

# 🟡 Practice 8 — Progressions in Different Keys

This is where the bucket becomes much more powerful.

Don't stay in C major forever.

You need to learn:

> **Roman numerals are movable.**

For example:

### I–V–vi–IV

In C:

`C – G – Am – F`

In G:

`G – D – Em – C`

In D:

`D – A – Bm – G`

In A:

`A – E – F#m – D`

In E:

`E – B – C#m – A`

### Your practice method

Don't immediately try all 12 keys.

Start with:

**C → G → D → A → E**

For each key:

1. Identify the I chord.
2. Build the V chord.
3. Build the vi chord.
4. Build the IV chord.
5. Play the progression.

For example:

### Key of G

`I–V–vi–IV`

↓

`G–D–Em–C`

### 🎯 Goal

Eventually you should see:

`I–V–vi–IV`

and think:

> "I don't care what key I'm in. I know the pattern."

That's true harmonic understanding.

`C → G → C`

> What happened?

Answer:

**I → V → I**

### Level 2

Play:

`C → F → G → C`

Answer:

**G / V**

### Level 3

Play:

`C → G → Am → F`

`I → V → vi → IV`

Have someone else play a progression.

### Very important

Don't worry if you cannot identify it immediately.

Your ear needs repeated exposure.

This is exactly like recognizing spoken words—you become faster through repetition.
