## Bucket 7.3 — 🎼 Arpeggios

By completing these 8 practices, you should be able to:

## 🎼 7.3.1 — 1–3–5–8 Pattern

### 🎯 Goal: Understand the basic arpeggio structure

An arpeggio is basically a chord played **one note at a time**.

For C Major:

**C → E → G → C**

Here:

* **1 = C** → Root
* **3 = E** → Major 3rd
* **5 = G** → 5th
* **8 = C** → Octave

### 🖐️ Right-hand practice

Use:

**1 → 2 → 3 → 5**

Play:

**C → E → G → C**

Then come back:

**C → G → E → C**

Don't worry about speed yet.

### 🔁 Practice

Start very slowly:

`C → E → G → C`

Pause.

Then:

`C → E → G → C → G → E → C`

Repeat **10 times**.

### 🎯 Focus on

* Every note should sound clearly.
* Don't hit two keys together.
* Keep your fingers relaxed.
* Try to make the spacing between notes equal.

### ✅ Move on when

You can play **C–E–G–C–G–E–C** 5 times without stopping or hitting wrong notes.

---

# 🎼 7.3.2 — Ascending Arpeggios

### 🎯 Goal: Develop upward finger movement

Now concentrate specifically on moving **low → high**.

Start with:

**C → E → G → C**

Then try:

**F → A → C → F**

Then:

**G → B → D → G**

### 🖐️ Practice method

Play each arpeggio slowly:

**C–E–G–C**

Pause.

**C–E–G–C**

Pause.

Repeat **5 times**.

Then remove the pause.

### 🔁 Exercise

Play:

`C–E–G–C` × 5

`F–A–C–F` × 5

`G–B–D–G` × 5

### 🎯 Focus on

Think:

**LOW → HIGH**

Don't rush the final octave.

Your hand should travel naturally rather than jumping aggressively between keys.

### ✅ Move on when

You can play all three major arpeggios ascending with **even timing and no hesitation**.

---

# 🎼 7.3.3 — Descending Arpeggios

### 🎯 Goal: Develop downward control

Now reverse everything.

Instead of:

**C → E → G → C**

play:

**C → G → E → C**

This develops control when your hand travels back toward the lower notes.

### 🔁 Practice

#### C Major

`C–G–E–C` × 5

#### F Major

`F–C–A–F` × 5

#### G Major

`G–D–B–G` × 5

### 🎯 Important

Don't think:

> "I'm just going backwards."

Think:

> **HIGH → MIDDLE → LOW**

Each note should still have equal importance.

### 🎯 Mini challenge

Play:

`C–E–G–C–G–E–C`

This combines **ascending + descending**.

Do this **10 times slowly**.

**1 – ♭3 – 5 – 8**

# 🎼 7.3.8 — Arpeggio Repetition

### 🎯 Goal: Develop consistency and control

This is where you turn everything you've learned into **muscle memory**.

You will practice the same arpeggios at different speeds.

### 🐢 Level 1 — Very slow

Play:

`C–E–G–C–G–E–C`

Focus entirely on accuracy.

× 5

---

### 🚶 Level 2 — Comfortable

Increase the speed slightly.

`C–E–G–C–G–E–C`

× 10

Your notes should remain evenly spaced.

---

### 🏃 Level 3 — Faster

Now increase speed again.

But remember:

**Speed comes after accuracy.**

If your notes become uneven, slow down.

---

### 🔄 Level 4 — Change keys

Cycle through:

`C Major → F Major → G Major`

Then:

`C Minor → F Minor → G Minor`

Start slowly.
