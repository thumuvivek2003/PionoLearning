# 🟦 LEVEL 2.1 — FINGER NUMBERS & HAND AWARENESS

## First: fix the numbering permanently

Both hands use the **same numbering system**:

| Finger | Number |
| ------ | -----: |
| Thumb  |  **1** |
| Index  |  **2** |
| Middle |  **3** |
| Ring   |  **4** |
| Little |  **5** |

So:

**Right hand:** 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5
**Left hand:** 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 when moving physically from left to right.

But importantly:

> Finger **3 is always your middle finger**, regardless of hand.

Don't create separate numbering systems for RH/LH.

---

# 🪣 2.1.1 — Finger Number Recognition

Before touching the keyboard, train your brain.

Look at your right hand and randomly point to fingers.

For example:

> "Which finger?"

You point:

**Thumb → 1**

**Ring → 4**

**Index → 2**

**Little → 5**

**Middle → 3**

Do this quickly.

### Then reverse it

Think:

> **4**

Immediately move your awareness to your **ring finger**.

Then:

> **2**

→ index.

> **5**

→ little.

> **1**

→ thumb.

### Target

You shouldn't need to consciously count:

> thumb → index → middle...

It should become:

> **3 = middle**

instantly.

# 🪣 2.1.2 — Finger Tapping

Put your right hand comfortably on a table.

Don't press hard.

Now:

**1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5**

Tap each finger individually.

Then:

**5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1**

Then random:

**1 → 3 → 5 → 2 → 4**

Then:

**4 → 1 → 5 → 3 → 2**

### Important

Don't make the other fingers fly into the air.

If you're tapping **3**, try to keep:

**1, 2, 4, 5 relaxed.**

You're teaching your brain:

> "I can command ONE finger without accidentally commanding the others."

That's the beginning of independence.

---

# 🪣 2.1.3 — Finger Lift Control

This one is VERY important for piano.

Rest your hand naturally.

Now lift only:

### Drill A

**Finger 1**

Hold for 1 second → relax.

**Finger 2**

Hold → relax.

**Finger 3**

Hold → relax.

...

through 5.

Then reverse.

### Drill B — random

Ask yourself:

> "Lift finger 4."

Only 4 moves.

Then:

> "Lift finger 1."

Only 1 moves.

Then:

> "Lift finger 3."

Only 3 moves.

### What you're looking for

Not speed.

You're looking for:

> **precision + relaxation**

If lifting finger 3 causes finger 4 to rise dramatically, that's not failure.

That's exactly the weakness you're discovering.

---

# 🪣 2.1.4 — Relaxation Control

This is where many beginners accidentally build bad muscle memory.

Play a key.

For example:

**RH thumb → C**

Press C.

Then consciously ask:

> "What muscles am I still holding?"

Release unnecessary tension.

Your hand should not feel like:

> 🖐️ **"I am preparing to attack the keyboard."**

It should feel more like:

> 🖐️ **"I'm resting on the keyboard and controlling one movement."**

### Simple cycle

**Press → hold → release → relax**

Do this slowly.

C → C → C → C

Don't worry about rhythm yet.

# 🪣 2.1.6 — Don't Depend on Looking at Fingers

Eventually, you want:

> **Eyes → Note → Finger**

rather than:

> Eyes → Finger → Keyboard → Guess

So occasionally do this:

Place your RH in C position.

Look at the keyboard.

Play:

**C D E F G**

Then close/avert your eyes briefly and play a **known** sequence.

Don't force this for random notes yet.

You're just beginning to build:

> **finger location awareness**
