# LEVEL 5.8 — 🥁 CHORD CHANGES & RHYTHM

## 🎯 Before starting: Your basic setup

Use a simple progression first:

**C → Am → F → G**

For example:

* C = C–E–G
* Am = A–C–E
* F = F–A–C
* G = G–B–D

Start around **60 BPM**.

Count:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4 | 1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Your metronome should click on every beat.

---

# 5.8.1 — 🎵 One Chord Per Bar

### 🎯 Goal

Change chords **once every 4 beats** while keeping perfectly steady time.

### Example

| Bar | Beats   | Chord |
| --- | ------- | ----- |
| 1   | 1-2-3-4 | C     |
| 2   | 1-2-3-4 | Am    |
| 3   | 1-2-3-4 | F     |
| 4   | 1-2-3-4 | G     |

Then repeat.

### How to practice

Play C on beat **1** and hold it for all four beats.

Count:

**C → 1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Then change to Am exactly when the next bar begins.

**Am → 1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Continue:

**C | Am | F | G**

### Practice levels

**Level A — Right hand only**

Play the chords with your right hand.

**Level B — Left hand only**

Play the chord shape with your left hand.

**Level C — Both hands**

Play the chord together with both hands.

### 🎯 Success target

Do **8–10 consecutive loops** without:

* missing a chord change
* stopping
* speeding up
* slowing down

---

# 5.8.2 — 🎵 Two Chords Per Bar

Now we're going to double the speed of your chord changes.

### 🎯 Goal

Change chords every **2 beats**.

Example:

| Beat | Chord |
| ---- | ----- |
| 1–2  | C     |
| 3–4  | G     |
| 1–2  | Am    |
| 3–4  | F     |

So:

**C – G | Am – F**

### Count

**C → 1-2**

**G → 3-4**

**Am → 1-2**

**F → 3-4**

### Important

Don't rush the change.

The chord should change **on beat 3**.

### Practice

Start:

**40 BPM**

Then:

**50 BPM → 60 BPM → 70 BPM**

Only increase when your timing is clean.

### 🎯 Success target

Play:

**C – G | Am – F**

for **2 minutes continuously** without losing the pulse.

---

# 5.8.3 — 🥁 Metronome Chord Practice

This is one of the **most important exercises for your contest**.

The metronome becomes your timing teacher.

### 🎯 Goal

Learn to keep your internal timing even when changing chords.

Start at:

**60 BPM**

Play:

**C | Am | F | G**

One chord per bar.

### Exercise 1 — Slow

60 BPM × 2 minutes

### Exercise 2 — Medium

70 BPM × 2 minutes

### Exercise 3

80 BPM × 2 minutes

### Exercise 4

90 BPM × 2 minutes

### Rule

**Never increase BPM because you can play faster.**

Increase BPM because you can play **accurately**.

Your progression should feel:

> click → chord → click → chord → click → chord

rather than:

> chord → panic → chord → rush → chord 😄

### 🎯 Success target

At each BPM, complete **10 clean loops**.

---

# 5.8.4 — 🎵 Whole-Note Chords

Here you are learning **sustained harmony**.

### 🎯 Goal

Play the chord once and hold it for the entire bar.

Example:

**C ————— | Am ————— | F ————— | G —————**

Count:

**1 2 3 4**

Don't hit the chord again.

### Exercise

Play:

**C | Am | F | G**

for 5 minutes.

Listen carefully to whether the chord remains stable.

### Focus on

* relaxed fingers
* no unnecessary movement
* consistent sound
* changing exactly on beat 1

### 🎯 Contest benefit

This teaches you to provide a **stable harmonic foundation** while another hand plays melody.

---

# 5.8.5 — 🎵 Half-Note Chords

Now introduce rhythmic movement.

### 🎯 Goal

Play the chord **twice per bar**.

Count:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Play on:

**1 and 3**

Example:

**C → 1**

**C → 3**

Then:

**Am → 1**

**Am → 3**

So:

**C C | Am Am | F F | G G**

### Exercise

Start at:

**60 BPM**

Then:

**70 BPM**

Then:

**80 BPM**

### Variation

Once comfortable, try playing on:

**1 and 3**

Then:

**2 and 4**

This develops rhythmic awareness.

### 🎯 Success target

Play for **2–3 minutes** without accidentally adding extra hits.

---

# 5.8.6 — 🥁 Quarter-Note Chords

Now every beat gets a chord hit.

### 🎯 Goal

Play four chord hits per bar.

For C:

**C C C C**

Count:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Then:

**Am Am Am Am**

### Full progression

**C C C C | Am Am Am Am | F F F F | G G G G**

### Important

Don't attack every chord too hard.

Think:

**DA – DA – DA – DA**

not:

**BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM** 😄

### Practice stages

**60 BPM → 70 BPM → 80 BPM → 90 BPM**

### 🎯 Success target

Maintain identical spacing between all four hits.

This is where your **rhythm control** starts becoming much stronger.

---

# 5.8.7 — 🎹 Chord + Melody

This is a **major contest skill**.

Now you separate responsibilities between your hands.

### Right hand

Play a simple melody.

### Left hand

Play chords.

For example:

**Left hand:** C chord

**Right hand:** C–D–E–G–E

Then:

**Left hand:** Am

**Right hand:** A–C–E–G–E

### Start extremely slowly

Don't try to perform immediately.

First:

### Step 1

Right hand melody alone.

### Step 2

Left hand chords alone.

### Step 3

Put them together at **40 BPM**.

### Step 4

Increase to **50 BPM**.

### Step 5

Increase to **60 BPM**.

### The golden rule

If your melody stops when your chord changes, **slow down**.

The goal is:

> **Left hand = stable rhythm**
> **Right hand = independent melody**

### 🎯 Success target

Play **8 complete loops** without stopping either hand.

---

# 5.8.8 — 🎹 Smooth Chord Transitions

This is where we start making your playing sound **professional** rather than like separate disconnected chords.

### 🎯 Goal

Move to the **nearest possible chord shape**.

Instead of:

**C → Am**

jumping your whole hand around, look for notes that can remain close.

For example:

**C major**

C – E – G

to

**Am**

A – C – E

Notice:

**C and E are common notes.**

So you can minimize movement.

### Practice

Play:

**C → Am**

very slowly.

Watch your fingers.

Then:

**Am → F**

Then:

**F → G**

Then:

**G → C**

### Next level

Practice:

**C → Am → F → G → C**

while keeping your hand movement as small as possible.

### 🎯 Question to ask yourself

> **“Can I reach the next chord without moving my entire hand?”**

If yes, do it.

---

# 5.8.9 — 🔄 Progression Loop

Now we're building **automaticity**.

### 🎯 Goal

Repeat one progression until your hands no longer need conscious thought for every chord.

Use:

**C → Am → F → G**

### Round 1

One chord per bar.

**C | Am | F | G**

Repeat for **5 minutes**.

### Round 2

Half-note rhythm.

**C C | Am Am | F F | G G**

Repeat for **5 minutes**.

### Round 3

Quarter-note rhythm.

**C C C C | Am Am Am Am | F F F F | G G G G**

Repeat for **5 minutes**.

### Round 4

Chord + melody.

Repeat for **5 minutes**.

### The real objective

Eventually, you should be able to:

**talk → think about melody → look at audience → continue playing**

while your chord progression continues automatically.

That's what **automaticity** means here.

---

# 5.8.10 — ⚡ Speed Drill

This is your final stage.

### 🎯 Goal

Increase speed **without sacrificing accuracy**.

Don't immediately jump from 60 to 120 BPM.

Use a ladder.

### Example

| Stage | BPM |  Time |
| ----- | --: | ----: |
| 1     |  50 | 2 min |
| 2     |  60 | 2 min |
| 3     |  70 | 2 min |
| 4     |  80 | 2 min |
| 5     |  90 | 2 min |
| 6     | 100 | 2 min |
| 7     | 110 | 2 min |

If you make repeated mistakes at 90 BPM:

**Go back to 80 BPM.**

Don't force 90.

### 🎯 The 3-speed rule

Find your:

**Comfortable BPM**

**Challenge BPM**

**Maximum clean BPM**

For example:

> Comfortable = 70
> Challenge = 80
> Maximum clean = 90

Your training should mostly happen around **70–80**, not constantly at your maximum.
