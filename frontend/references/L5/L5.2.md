# 🟢 SESSION 1 — C Major

### 🎯 Goal

Instantly recognize and play:

**C Major = C–E–G**

### Step 1 — Understand the formula

Every major chord follows:

**1 – 3 – 5**

For C major:

* 1 = C
* 3 = E
* 5 = G

Therefore:

**C–E–G**

### Step 2 — Find it on the keyboard

Locate **C**.

Then play:

**C → E → G**

Play all three together.

### Step 3 — Practice slowly

Play:

**C-E-G → release**

Repeat **10 times**.

Then play the notes individually:

**C → E → G → E → C**

### Step 4 — Random recognition

Close your eyes.

Ask yourself:

> “C Major?”

Answer:

> **C-E-G**

Then play it.

### Step 5 — Speed drill

Set a timer for **2 minutes**.

Randomly alternate:

**C Major → C-E-G**

Try to reduce the thinking time.

### ✅ Session target

You should be able to hear/see **C Major** and play **C-E-G within 2–3 seconds**.

# 🟡 SESSION 8 — Sharp Major Chords

Now we move to the **five major chords whose roots are black keys**.

These are:

| Chord    | Notes    |
| -------- | -------- |
| C# Major | C#–F–G#  |
| D# Major | D#–G–A#  |
| F# Major | F#–A#–C# |
| G# Major | G#–C–D#  |
| A# Major | A#–D–F   |

### ⚠️ Important

You will often see these written with enharmonic spellings.

For example:

**C# Major = C#–E#–G#**

Since **E# is the same physical key as F**, beginners often encounter:

**C#–F–G#**

on the keyboard.

For your contest, learn the **musical spelling** as well as the physical keyboard location.

# 🟡 SESSION 9 — Flat Naming & Enharmonic Names

This session is about understanding that the **same physical piano key can have different names**.

For example:

**C# = D♭**

They are the same piano key physically.

Similarly:

* D# = E♭
* F# = G♭
* G# = A♭
* A# = B♭

### Why this matters

A contest may say:

> **A♭ Major**

instead of:

> **G# Major**

You need to understand that these are enharmonic equivalents.

For example:

**A♭ Major = A♭–C–E♭**

while the equivalent physical keys correspond to:

**G#–C–D#**

### Practice

Take each black key:

**C#/D♭**

**D#/E♭**

**F#/G♭**

**G#/A♭**

**A#/B♭**

Say both names aloud.

### 🎯 Goal

When you see:

> **G♭**

your brain should know:

> Same physical key as **F#**
