## 🎹 4.7.1 — A Natural Minor Notes

### Notes:

**A – B – C – D – E – F – G – A**

First, don't play anything. Just **find the notes**.

### Step 1 — Locate A

On your keyboard:

**A → B → C → D → E → F → G → A**

Notice something important:

> 🎯 **A Natural Minor uses ONLY white keys.**

There are **no sharps or flats**.

### Step 2 — Say the notes aloud

Point to each key and say:

> **A – B – C – D – E – F – G – A**

Then descend:

> **A – G – F – E – D – C – B – A**

Do this several times.

### Step 3 — Random recognition

Now don't always start from A.

Point randomly and ask yourself:

* What comes after C?
* What comes before F?
* Where is G?
* What note is between D and F?
* What is the octave above A?

### 🎯 Practice target

You should eventually look at the keyboard and immediately recognize:

**A B C D E F G A**

without counting keys.

# 🎼 4.7.2 — A Minor Formula

Now understand **why these notes are A minor**.

The natural minor formula is:

> **W – H – W – W – H – W – W**

Where:

* **W = Whole step**
* **H = Half step**

Starting from A:

**A → B = W**

**B → C = H**

**C → D = W**

**D → E = W**

**E → F = H**

**F → G = W**

**G → A = W**

So:

**A —W— B —H— C —W— D —W— E —H— F —W— G —W— A**

### 🎯 Important observation

The half steps occur between:

> **B–C**
> **E–F**

This is exactly why A natural minor can use all white keys.

### Practice drill

Play slowly and say:

> A → B **whole**
> B → C **half**
> C → D **whole**
> D → E **whole**
> E → F **half**
> F → G **whole**
> G → A **whole**

Don't worry about speed.

### 🎯 Learning outcome

You should be able to answer immediately:

**Q: What is the natural minor formula?**

**A: W-H-W-W-H-W-W**

And:

**Q: What are the half steps in A minor?**

**A: B-C and E-F**

# 🎼 4.7.3 — A Minor vs C Major

This is a **VERY important concept**, buddy.

### C Major:

**C – D – E – F – G – A – B – C**

### A Natural Minor:

**A – B – C – D – E – F – G – A**

Look carefully.

They contain **exactly the same seven notes**:

> **A B C D E F G**

The difference is where the scale **starts and centers**.

### 🎯 This is called Relative Minor

**A minor is the relative minor of C major.**

And:

**C major is the relative major of A minor.**

You don't need to memorize complicated theory yet.

Just remember:

> **C Major = A B C D E F G**
> **A Minor = A B C D E F G**

Same notes.

Different tonal center.

---

## 🎧 Sound comparison exercise

Play:

**C → D → E → F → G → A → B → C**

Listen to the feeling.

Then play:

**A → B → C → D → E → F → G → A**

Listen again.

Even though the keys are the same, the **musical center/feeling changes**.

### 🎯 Your goal

Be able to say:

> **"A minor uses the same notes as C major, but A is the tonal center."**

That's the key concept.

# 🎹 4.7.4 — RH A Minor

Now we actually play the scale.

For **Right Hand**, use:

### Ascending

**A B C D E F G A**

Fingering:

**1 2 3 1 2 3 4 5**

So:

| Note      |  A |  B |  C |  D |  E |  F |  G |  A |
| --------- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| RH Finger |  1 |  2 |  3 |  1 |  2 |  3 |  4 |  5 |

The important movement is:

> **A B C → thumb under → D E F G A**

### Descending

Use:

**5 4 3 2 1 3 2 1**

So:

**A G F E D C B A**

### Practice Method

#### Round 1 — Very slow

Play each note separately.

**A — B — C — D — E — F — G — A**

Don't worry about rhythm.

#### Round 2 — Connect the notes

Try to make it sound like:

**A-B-C-D-E-F-G-A**

rather than eight separate key presses.

#### Round 3 — Descend

**A-G-F-E-D-C-B-A**

#### Round 4 — Up + Down

**A-B-C-D-E-F-G-A-G-F-E-D-C-B-A**

### 🎯 Main focus

Don't chase speed.

Focus on:

* Correct fingering
* Relaxed hand
* Even volume
* No hesitation at thumb crossing
* Smooth ascending
* Smooth descending

# 🎹 4.7.6 — Hands Together

🚨 **Don't jump into this too quickly.**

You should first be reasonably comfortable with RH and LH separately.

Now play both hands simultaneously.

### Ascending

Both hands play:

**A → B → C → D → E → F → G → A**

But the fingers are different.

### RH

**1 2 3 1 2 3 4 5**

### LH

**5 4 3 2 1 3 2 1**

So your hands look like:

| Note |  A |  B |  C |  D |  E |  F |  G |  A |
| ---- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| RH   |  1 |  2 |  3 |  1 |  2 |  3 |  4 |  5 |
| LH   |  5 |  4 |  3 |  2 |  1 |  3 |  2 |  1 |
