# 🎼 LEVEL 7.1 — 🎵 MELODIES

## 7.1.1 — 🎹 Right-Hand Melodies

**Goal:** Build confidence playing melodies with your right hand.

### Practice Session — 15 minutes

**Step 1 — Note warm-up (3 min)**

* Choose 5 nearby notes, e.g. **C–D–E–F–G**.
* Play them slowly ascending and descending.
* Say the note names while playing.
* Keep your fingers relaxed.

**Step 2 — Simple 4-bar melody (5 min)**
Choose a very easy melody using mainly C–D–E–F–G.

Example:

`C D E E | D C D - | E F G G | F E D -`

* Play with RH fingers **1-2-3-3 | 2-1-2 | 3-4-5-5 | 4-3-2**
* Start very slowly.
* Do not stop when you make a mistake.
* Restart only after completing the phrase.

**Step 3 — Rhythm practice (3 min)**

* Play the melody with a metronome.
* Start around **50–60 BPM**.
* One note per beat.
* Focus on staying with the beat.

**Step 4 — Smoothness (4 min)**
Play the same melody:

1. Very slowly
2. Medium speed
3. Without looking constantly at your right hand

### Move forward when:

You can play a **4–8 bar melody 3 times consecutively without stopping**.

# 7.1.2 — 🎹 Left-Hand Melodies

**Goal:** Make your left hand capable of playing melodies independently.

### Practice Session — 15 minutes

**Step 1 — LH finger warm-up (3 min)**

Play:

`C-D-E-F-G-F-E-D-C`

Using:

`5-4-3-2-1-2-3-4-5`

Go slowly.

**Step 2 — Simple melody (5 min)**

Use a melody containing only 5 notes.

For example:

`C D E | E D C | D E F | E D C`

Play entirely with the **left hand**.

**Step 3 — Eyes-off practice (3 min)**

Look at the music rather than your fingers.

Your objective is to develop **keyboard awareness**.

**Step 4 — Rhythm + accuracy (4 min)**

Use a metronome at **50 BPM**.

Play the melody 3–5 times.

### Important rule:

Don't try to make the LH fast.

Your priority is:

**Correct note → correct finger → correct rhythm → smoothness → speed**

### Move forward when:

You can play a short melody with your LH while **mostly looking at the music instead of the keyboard**.

---

# 7.1.3 — 🎵 Stepwise Melodies

**Goal:** Instantly recognize notes that move to neighboring keys.

Stepwise movement means:

`C → D → E → F → G`

or

`G → F → E → D → C`

### Practice Session — 15 minutes

**Step 1 — Ascending steps (3 min)**

Play:

`C D E F G`

Then:

`D E F G A`

Then:

`E F G A B`

**Step 2 — Descending steps (3 min)**

Play:

`G F E D C`

Then:

`A G F E D`

**Step 3 — Random step patterns (4 min)**

Practice patterns such as:

`C D E D E F E D`

`G F E F G A G F`

Try to recognize the movement **before playing**.

**Step 4 — Melody application (5 min)**

Take a simple melody and identify:

* ↑ = moving upward
* ↓ = moving downward
* → = repeated note

For example:

`C D E E D C`

Think:

`↑ ↑ → ↓ ↓`

before playing.

### Move forward when:

You can see a stepwise passage and play it **without consciously searching for every individual key**.

---

# 7.1.4 — 🎵 Skipping Melodies

**Goal:** Improve accuracy when the melody jumps over notes.

Stepwise:

`C → D`

Skipping:

`C → E`

Larger skip:

`C → G`

### Practice Session — 15 minutes

**Step 1 — 3rd skips (4 min)**

Practice:

`C E D F E G F A`

Then descending:

`G E F D E C`

**Step 2 — 4th/5th skips (4 min)**

Practice:

`C F D G E A`

Then:

`G C F D A E`

Play slowly.

**Step 3 — Mixed movement (4 min)**

Practice:

`C D E G F E C`

This combines:

**steps + skips + steps + skips**

**Step 4 — Short melody (3 min)**

Take a melody containing jumps and play it without looking down after every jump.

### Golden rule:

**Prepare your hand for the destination note before you move.**

Don't stare at the current key.

Look ahead.

### Move forward when:

You can accurately play a melody containing skips at **60–70 BPM** without repeatedly stopping.

---

# 7.1.5 — 🔁 Repeated Notes

**Goal:** Develop finger control, rhythm and evenness.

Repeated notes look like:

`C C C C`

or:

`E E E — E E`

### Practice Session — 15 minutes

**Step 1 — Single-note repetition (3 min)**

Play:

`C C C C`

Then:

`D D D D`

Then:

`E E E E`

Keep every note the **same volume**.

**Step 2 — Finger repetition (4 min)**

Try:

`C C C C | D D D D | E E E E`

Use controlled finger movements.

Avoid hitting the keys aggressively.

**Step 3 — Rhythm variations (4 min)**

Practice:

`C C C C`

Then:

`C — C —`

Then:

`C C — C`

Then:

`C C C —`

**Step 4 — Melody application (4 min)**

Use a melody containing repeated notes.

Focus on:

* even timing
* even volume
* relaxed fingers
* no accidental extra notes

### Move forward when:

Your repeated notes sound **even and controlled**, rather than rushed or uneven.

---

# 7.1.6 — 🎵 Melody with Different Rhythms

**Goal:** Connect your note reading with rhythm reading.

This is extremely important because a melody isn't just **which notes** you play.

It is:

**Which note + when to play it + how long to hold it.**

### Practice Session — 20 minutes

### Step 1 — Quarter notes (3 min)

Count:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Play one note per count.

Example:

`C D E F`

---

### Step 2 — Half notes (3 min)

Count:

**1-2 | 3-4**

Example:

`C — | D —`

Hold each note for 2 beats.

---

### Step 3 — Whole notes (3 min)

Count:

**1-2-3-4**

Example:

`C — — —`

Hold for the entire measure.

---

### Step 4 — Eighth notes (5 min)

Count:

**1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &**

Play:

`C D E F G A B C`

with two notes per beat.

---

### Step 5 — Mix rhythms (6 min)

Now combine them.

Example:

`C D E— | F G A B | C———`

Don't rush the short notes and don't shorten the long notes.

### Move forward when:

You can play the melody while **counting the rhythm correctly without losing your place**.

---

# 7.1.7 — 🧠 Short Melody Memorization

**Goal:** Develop the ability to play a melody without constantly looking at the keyboard or music.

This is particularly valuable for performance.

### Practice Session — 20 minutes

Choose a **4–8 bar melody**.

### Step 1 — Observe (2 min)

Before playing, identify:

* Starting note
* Ending note
* Highest note
* Lowest note
* Stepwise sections
* Skips
* Repeated notes
* Rhythm patterns

Don't immediately play it.

**Read it first.**

---

### Step 2 — Play while reading (5 min)

Play extremely slowly.

Focus on accuracy.

Don't worry about speed.

---

### Step 3 — Divide into phrases (4 min)

Break the melody into small chunks.

For example:

**Phrase A:** bars 1–2
**Phrase B:** bars 3–4
**Phrase C:** bars 5–6
**Phrase D:** bars 7–8

Master each phrase separately.

---

### Step 4 — Memory test (4 min)

Look at the music.

Play Phrase A.

Then close/cover the music and try again.

Repeat with B, C and D.

---

### Step 5 — Full performance (5 min)

Now play the entire melody **without looking at the keys constantly**.

Your eyes should increasingly stay on:

**music → ahead of your hands → keyboard only when necessary**

### Move forward when:

You can play the complete **4–8 bar melody from memory 3 times consecutively** with correct notes and rhythm.

## ⭐ The progression you should follow

Don't think:

**"I need to play fast."**

Think:

**👀 Read → 🎹 Find → 🎵 Play → 🥁 Keep rhythm → 🔄 Repeat → 🚀 Increase speed**

And for your contest preparation, use this rule throughout Level 7:

> **Accuracy → Rhythm → Smoothness → Memory → Speed**

If you build these seven skills properly, **7.1 becomes the foundation for much harder melody playing later.**
